Oli is an AI-powered agronomist for small and medium farmers worldwide. Ask a question or upload a photo of a sick plant — Oli diagnoses diseases, recommends organic and chemical treatments with exact dosages, and follows up to confirm the outcome.

## Key Features

- **Photo Diagnosis** — Upload a photo, get an instant diagnosis with confidence score
- **Treatment Plans** — Organic and chemical options with exact product names, dosages, and frequencies
- **Every Crop** — Olives, vines, citrus, tomatoes, vegetables, fruit trees, cereals, and more
- **VIO Follow-up** — Oli checks back after treatment to confirm it worked
- **Field Memory** — Saves your crop profiles and intervention history
- **24/7 Availability** — No waiting for an agronomist callback
- **450+ Diseases** — Diseases, pests, and nutrient deficiencies identified
